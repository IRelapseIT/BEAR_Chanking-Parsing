
 Usage: infinity_emb v2 [OPTIONS]

 Multiple Model CLI Playbook:
 - 1. cli options can be overloaded i.e. `v2 --model-id model/id1 --model-id
 model/id2 --batch-size 8 --batch-size 4`
 - 2. or adapt the defaults by setting ENV Variables separated by `;`:
 INFINITY_MODEL_ID="model/id1;model/id2;" && INFINITY_BATCH_SIZE="8;4;"
 - 3. single items are broadcasted to `--model-id` length, making `v2
 --model-id model/id1 --model-id/id2 --batch-size 8` both models have
 batch-size 8.
